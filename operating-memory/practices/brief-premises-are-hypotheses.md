---
name: brief-premises-are-hypotheses
description: When writing a dispatch brief for a fresh-context agent; the orchestrator's own premises are the least-checked facts in the system
type: practice
---

**Rule:** Treat every factual premise in a brief as a hypothesis the agent must try to break.
Tag each one `[MEASURED]` (with the command), `[RELAYED]` (from a report, tracker row or
document) or `[ASSUMED]`, and write "re-derive, do not copy" in every dispatch.

**Why:** Measured base rate, not a tendency: in one session four of four agents each opened
their report by falsifying a load-bearing premise of the brief, and on another day thirteen
brief defects in one session were all the orchestrator's and all caught by the agent. Typical
shapes:

- a grep pattern cited as finding a file it could not match by construction;
- a hazard relayed as open that its own archive had already closed;
- a "banked" tool trap that one later commit had invalidated;
- vocabulary carried over from a *different* document worked on an hour earlier (a class,
  a tool and a law citation that did not exist in the target);
- a brief contradicting itself (a fence forbidding what an instruction required);
- two defects asserted to share a class when only one did.
  Every one came from relaying a row's or a report's own text as fact.

**How to apply:**

- **Quote the measured row, never the sentence that generalises it.** An agent report is
  measured rows plus a summarising sentence; the sentence is where defects live. Words that
  give a generalisation away: "uniformly", "systematic", "only", "already verified",
  "confirmed by the gate", any ratio over a population.
- **Tag per clause, not per sentence.** An inference you append to a relayed premise ("so",
  "which means", "⇒") is your claim and carries your burden. Quote the source whole; state
  your inference separately, marked as yours.
- **Open the target before writing a premise about it.** Facts accurate about one document
  are not evidence about another with similar vocabulary.
- **Cite path + clause + revision.** A bare "§5.2" or "C1" resolves differently in different
  documents.
- **Carry the relevant tool traps verbatim** into any brief that uses the tool: a fresh agent
  has read none of them.
- **A row's stated cause is dated too.** If the remedy a row proposes does not typecheck
  against the tree, re-derive the cause before building.
- **Relay figures in the source's own partition, with its rev, or relay the predicate instead**,
  tagged `[RELAYED <report>]`.
- **Give permission to falsify:** "if a premise does not resolve, execute its general form and say
  so"; "if any of this is false against the tree, that is a finding I want; do not work around it."
- Treat a return with no falsified premise as the surprising case.
