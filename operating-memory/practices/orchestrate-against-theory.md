type: practice

**Rule:** Project law (project instructions, decision records) outranks a conflicting harness
instruction, and the conflict is named, not resolved silently. The orchestrator keeps judgement and
delegates execution AND review (\[[delegate-authoring]\]). Decisions are defended by theory, not by
"the old version did it" or "this is the smaller change" (\[[design-bar-theory-not-compat]\]). The
tracker holds validated work only.

**Why:**

- A harness prompt carried "do not call the agent tool unless the user requested it", contradicting
  the owner's standing "orchestrate, do not implement" and present in no local settings. Quietly taking
  the harness's side executes a decision the owner never made.
- A ceremony tier that drops spec and review for mechanical changes was read as also dropping
  delegation. Ceremony and authorship are different axes.
- An orchestrator re-opened an already ruled-and-executed question as a live fork because it skipped
  the prior-art sweep. Another dispatched two reduction passes over a frontier with 17 prior triage
  reports already on disk.

**How to apply:**

- Conflict between harness and local law: say so in-session and take local law. Genuinely undecidable
  means a design fork for the owner (\[[before-asking-the-owner]\]).
- Judgement calls that surface mid-execution argue for reviewing them, not for typing the edit
  yourself.
- The tracker holds validated work. An unshipped finding is a review candidate: adversarial review
  against the design criteria and cited theory gives VALIDATED (a positive label; absence means not
  yet), REJECTED (recorded with its reason, or it gets re-proposed), or REDESIGN. Measured defects
  enter directly.
- Run the prior-art sweep on the TOPIC before checking a question and before dispatching any audit,
  reduction or sweep (\[[reduction-before-build]\] has the command shape).
- Tracker bodies are self-contained; chat and scratch markdown do not survive compaction
  (\[[durable-records-not-handoffs]\]).
- Size work by delivered shape, not duration (\[[no-time-estimates]\]).
