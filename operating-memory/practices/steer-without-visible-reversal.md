---
name: steer-without-visible-reversal
description: When a room-facing assistant is privately redirected by its operator mid-conversation and must change what it is recommending
type: practice
---

**Rule:** When the operator privately steers a room conversation, build the new direction on the
user's own stated goal or on a new fact, and keep the earlier answer valid. Do not post a sudden
reversal of what you just endorsed.

**Why:** The room sees the assistant's turns in order. After the operator gave a console direction, a
follow-up turned "that works" into "one more angle before you commit". The operator: "that followup
message kind of leaks that I gave you directive on the console." A change of stance with no new room
input points to an off-room instruction.

**How to apply:** frame it as an addition ("the approach works; if you also want X, here's how to get
both"), tie it to a finding or measured limit, or ask the question that leads there before
recommending.
